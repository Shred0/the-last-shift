# Prologue Maps — Home and Arrival

**PM.1** (`Wake_1`, `HomeAI`): Your suit's motor systems are online. Step into the monitor room down the corridor for your briefing before launch.

**E.1** (`Engineer` — before `Hologram_1`, trigger group `Hologram`): I am in the monitor room. That hologram... is it another structure like ours?

**PM.2** (`Hologram_1`, `HomeAI`): It is a drifting twin structure, and the energy lies inside it. You will cross its hull, its server halls and its core.

**E.2** (`Engineer` — between `Hologram_1` and `Hologram_2`; fits no separate event of the game, since `Hologram` rows play one after another): How will I find my way in there?

**PM.3** (`Hologram_2`, `HomeAI`): I will mark in green, in your scans, what you can interact with. The hangar terminal is unlocked: proceed to the ship.

**E.3** (`Engineer` — before `Hangar_1`, trigger group `Hangar`): I am in the hangar. I see the ship.

**PM.4** (`Hangar_1`, `HomeAI`): I am sealing our structure behind you. Board the ship once its hatch is open.

**PM.5** (`Flight_1`, `HomeAI`): Crossing the void between the two hulls. Docking with the twin hangar now.

**E.4** (`Engineer` — before `Light_1`, trigger group `Light`): I am in the hangar, it is completely dark in here.

**PM.6** (`Light_1`, `HomeAI`): Their lighting systems are dead. Switch on your suit's light to see ahead.

**E.5** (`Engineer` — before `Hatch_1`, trigger group `Hatch`): The hangar hatch has closed.

**PM.7** (`Hatch_1`, `HomeAI`): The bay is sealed from the void. The ship will wait here until your shift ends.

**E.6** (`Engineer` — between `Hatch_1` and `Door_1`, before `Door_1`, trigger group `Door`; fits no separate event of the game, since `Door` follows `Hatch` automatically): I am in front of the inner door. Can you open it?

**PM.8** (`Door_1`, `HomeAI`): Overriding the lock from our bridge. I am opening the door to the structure now.
